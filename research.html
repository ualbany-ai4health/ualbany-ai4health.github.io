How we work

How we keep a model honest.

RecordSplitTrainTest
Step 1 · Record

Every sample keeps its patient's ID.

A study has dozens of people and thousands of 30-second sleep epochs or MRI slices from each. No sample ever loses track of whose body it came from.

Step 2 · Split

Split by person, before anything else.

Whole patients go to the test group. If slices of one brain sat on both sides, a model could recognise the person instead of the disease.

Step 3 · Train

Every choice is made inside training.

Architecture, features and thresholds are tuned on training patients only, in nested folds. The test group stays sealed.

Step 4 · Test

The score comes from strangers.

The reported number is measured once, on patients the model has never seen. That is the number a clinic would get.
